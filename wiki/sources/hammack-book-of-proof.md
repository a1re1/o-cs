# Book of Proof (Hammack, 3rd ed.)

## What it is
A bridge-course text: Part I Fundamentals (sets, logic, counting), Part II How to Prove Conditional
Statements (direct proof, contrapositive), Part III More on Proof (contradiction, non-conditional
statements, existence and uniqueness, [[induction]]), Part IV Relations, Functions and Cardinality.
Each proof technique gets a template ("Outline for proof by contrapositive"), worked examples, and
many exercises with solutions to odd problems.

## What it adds
- Explicit *outlines* for each technique, which [[proof-techniques]] adopts.
- A careful treatment of "if and only if" proofs, existence/uniqueness, and disproof by counterexample.
- Cardinality (countable vs uncountable, Cantor's diagonal argument) presented as a proof exercise,
  which is the on-ramp to computability in §1.5/§5.1 (see [[sets-relations-functions]]).
