# Linear Algebra (Hefferon, 4th ed.)

## What it is
Free text with every exercise solved. Chapters: Linear Systems (Gauss's method, reduced echelon form),
Vector Spaces (subspaces, span, basis, dimension), Maps Between Spaces (isomorphism, homomorphism,
matrices as representations, change of basis, projection), Determinants, Similarity (complex vector
spaces, diagonalizability, eigenvalues, Jordan form). Topics sections on Markov chains, PageRank-style
ranking, and linear recurrences.

## What it adds
- Proofs that a matrix *represents* a linear map w.r.t. chosen bases, and that change of basis is
  similarity — the abstraction [[matrices-and-linear-maps]] leans on.
- Careful treatment of reduced row echelon form uniqueness ([[gaussian-elimination-lu]]).
- Nothing on SVD or numerics; use [[strang-18-06]] and [[boyd-vmls]] for those.
