# Software Engineering Track
**In one sentence.** For those who build and maintain real software systems: master
construction, testing, architecture, operations, and the social practice of open source.
Prerequisite: [[core-spine]].

   [[property-based-testing]], [[fuzzing]],

3. **Architecture & design** — [[software-architecture-and-system-design]],
   [[api-design]], [[microservices-and-resilience-patterns]],
   [[scalable-system-design]]; and DDIA (see [[distributed-databases-and-nosql]]).
4. **DevOps & SRE** — [[continuous-integration-and-delivery]],
   [[containers-and-kubernetes]], [[infrastructure-as-code-and-devops]],
   [[observability-monitoring-and-incident-response]].
5. **Web & mobile** — [[web-backends-sessions-and-authentication]],
   [[frontend-frameworks-and-state-management]], [[mobile-development-and-cross-platform]].
6. **Open-source practice** — [[open-source-practice-and-governance]],
   [[dependency-management-and-packaging]], [[software-supply-chain-security]].

   [[technical-debt-and-maintenance]].
