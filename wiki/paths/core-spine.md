# Core Spine — The Path Everyone Takes
**In one sentence.** The ordered backbone of computer science: master these in sequence
and you can enter any of the specialized tracks ([[systems-track]], [[theory-track]],
[[ai-ml-track]], [[software-engineering-track]], [[human-centered-track]], [[data-track]]).

## What to study to learn computer science from scratch, in order

## Why this order
Each stage assumes the previous. Programming gives you something to reason about; discrete
math gives you the tools to reason; data structures and algorithms are the vocabulary of
efficiency; architecture and OS show what the machine really does; networks and databases
are the systems you'll actually build on; theory tells you what is and isn't possible.

## The spine (in order)
1. **Programming & abstraction** — write and decompose programs; [[data-abstraction]],
   [[managing-complexity-in-software-design]].
2. **Discrete mathematics** — [[proof-techniques]], [[induction]],
   [[proof-systems-and-natural-deduction]]; the language of correctness.
3. **Data structures** — [[arrays-and-linked-lists]], [[hash-tables]],
   [[binary-search-trees]], [[advanced-data-structures]].
4. **Algorithms & analysis** — [[asymptotic-notation]], [[divide-and-conquer]],
   [[greedy-algorithms]], [[dynamic-programming]], and hardness via
   [[np-completeness-and-reductions]].
5. **Computer systems / architecture** — [[caches-and-memory-hierarchy]],
   [[pipelining-and-hazards]]; how code becomes execution (CSAPP/15-213).
6. **Operating systems** — [[processes-and-threads]], [[virtual-memory]],
   [[cpu-scheduling]], [[file-systems]].
7. **Networking** — [[ip-routing-and-forwarding]],
   [[tcp-reliability-and-congestion-control]], [[dns-http-and-the-web-stack]].
8. **Databases** — [[relational-model]], [[transactions-and-concurrency-control]].
9. **Theory of computation** — [[finite-automata-and-regular-languages]],
   [[turing-machines]], [[complexity-classes]].
10. **Software construction** — [[software-testing-fundamentals]], [[refactoring]],
    plus command-line fluency ([[shell-and-unix-tools]]).

Then pick a 400-level track.
